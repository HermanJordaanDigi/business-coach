## 9. Statistical Insights

### Correlation Analysis

**Strong Correlations Found:**
- **Revenue ↔ Cash Collected:** r = 0.997 (Very Strong Positive)
  - As expected, higher revenue directly correlates with higher cash collection
  - Validates data integrity

**Weak/No Significant Correlations:**
- Revenue ↔ Upsell: r = 0.099 (No correlation)

**Key Insight:** Upsells occur relatively independently of deal size, suggesting upsell success is more about process/technique than customer spending capacity.
